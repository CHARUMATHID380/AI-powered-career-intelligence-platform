Intake procedure

Three steps, no manual tagging

The same pipeline runs whether you paste raw text or drop in a file.

01 — Submit

Paste or upload

Drop in a PDF, Word doc, or plain text file — or paste the résumé text directly. Nothing is stored beyond your session.

02 — Extract

Text & skills pulled out

The document is parsed, cleaned, and scanned against a skills vocabulary so key terms surface automatically.

03 — Match

Ranked against every category

The classifier scores the résumé against each job category and returns a ranked list with confidence percentages.

On file

Two classifiers, kept deliberately separate

A broad reader for general screening, and a specialist panel for three high-volume roles.

General screening

Broad classifier

A logistic regression model trained across the full category set. This is the default reader for any résumé, regardless of role.

  • MethodLogistic Regression
  • Coverage8 job categories
  • OutputTop 5 ranked, with skills
Specialist panel

Narrow classifier

A Random Forest and XGBoost ensemble trained on a hand-labeled set, focused on three roles where precision matters most.

  • MethodRandom Forest + XGBoost
  • CoverageJava Dev · Business Analyst · PM
  • OutputTop 3, model-selectable
